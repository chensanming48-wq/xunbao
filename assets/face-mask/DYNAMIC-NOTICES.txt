dynamic-component.json pins exact dependency versions, download URLs, byte counts
and SHA-256 digests. Third-party wheel license and dist-info files are preserved
when installed. The existing NumPy, OpenCV, Pillow, packaging and six components
are reused without replacing their files or licenses.

tests/fixtures/face-canonical.json is a modified two-dimensional test fixture
derived from MediaPipe canonical_face_model.obj, under Apache License 2.0.
It preserves vertex order, drops depth, centers the bounding box, scales the
largest extent to 0.8, flips the vertical axis and rounds coordinates to 6 places.
It is used only by tests, not by the runtime or to infer user avatar positions.
